## 8. Lookahead safeguards in the clean layer

- `/plays` scores are recorded after the play. 115,517 of 1,944,845 plays (5.9%) have a post-play score different from the pre-snap score. The clean `offense_score`, `defense_score` and `score_diff` are pre-snap (previous play in the game); the post-play values keep an `_after` suffix and must not be used as state.
- `garbage_time` uses the pre-snap margin only.
- `games`: postgame columns (points, postgame Elo/WP, attendance, excitement, notes, completed) are listed in `cfb.leakage.GAME_POSTGAME_FIELDS`. `lines` carries `home_score`/`away_score` (postgame) for evaluation only.
- `team_coaching.coaching_change` uses only the coach who started the season; `in_season_change` is season-end information and never a same-season feature.
- `transfers.transfer_date` dates each portal entry, so transfer status can be applied as of a date.
- `player_seasons` is a roster snapshot pulled after (or, for 2026, during) the season: use it for static attributes (position, size, hometown), not for who was available on a given date.

## 9. Start season

- Offensive inputs complete (ppa ≥ 99%, passer ≥ 92%, receiver on completions ≥ 95%, rusher ≥ 90%): 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026. Not met: 2021.
- Incompletion targets ≥ 70% (any source): 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2025, 2026. Below 60%: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024.
- Defender credited on < 80% of sacks: 2014, 2015, 2020, 2021. Interceptor credited on < 80% of interceptions: 2021, 2022, 2023.

**Recommendation: keep 2014 as the start season.** Every input the offensive play model needs is complete from 2014 on, and the earliest seasons give the career and season effects more history. 2014 has no prior season of player data, so treat it as a burn-in season and evaluate the game model from 2015. Two gaps are season-specific rather than reasons to start later, and Phase 3 has to handle them explicitly: incompletion targets in 2021–2024, and defender credits in the seasons listed above.
