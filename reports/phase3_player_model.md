# Phase 3: Stage 1 player value model

Generated by `cfb player report`. All values are as of a fit date: the fit through week w-1 is the one used for week w games.

## 1. Model

```
ppa ~ StudentT(nu, mu, sigma[pass|rush])
pass: mu = a[pass,season] + ctx.b_pass + O[off] - D[def] + theta_pass[passer] + 1{target known}(k[season] + theta_recv[target])
rush: mu = a[rush,season] + ctx.b_rush + O[off] - D[def] + theta_rush[rusher]
theta_role[p,s,g] = alpha_role[position] + c[p] + s[p,s] + g[p,s,g]
c ~ N(X.gamma, tau_c)   X = recruit rating, stars, missing recruit, height, weight (within position), transferred (as of fit date)
s[first] = 0; s[next] = s[prev] + N(0, tau_s*sqrt(gap));  g ~ N(0, tau_g)
alpha coded relative to a reference position per role (QB passing, RB rushing, WR receiving)
within-season scales (tau_c, tau_g, unit tau_conf / tau_team0 / FCS scale) held at single-season NUTS fits on the 3 prior seasons
season-step scales (tau_s, unit tau_team / rho) held at two-season NUTS fits on the 3 prior season pairs
O, D = conference-season mean + AR(1) team deviation across seasons (FCS teams one pooled group, tighter spread)
```

- Data: 1,311,146 plays (model-eligible, garbage time excluded), 13 seasons. Context = down, distance bucket, yards-to-goal bucket, home/away/neutral.
- Missing attribution is not imputed: unknown passers and rushers get a season-level indicator, and unknown targets (2021-2024 incompletions) get no receiver term. Receiver values in 2021-2024 rest mostly on completions and are less reliable.
- Defense: the play model has only the team-season unit D. Individual defenders are valued by the box-score model (section 7). Kickers, punters and offensive linemen are out of scope.
- *Value* = c + s: EPA per play relative to the position average (alpha) in that role.

## 2. Inference check: NUTS vs SVI

Mean-field SVI biases hierarchical scales, so the as-of fits hold every scale at NUTS estimates (empirical Bayes) and SVI fits the effects. The checks below justify that choice.

### 2c. Scales used by the as-of fits

Every hierarchical scale is held at NUTS posterior means (empirical Bayes); SVI fits only the effects. The as-of fits for season S use the mean of the fits from the 3 seasons before S. Burn-in seasons (2014, 2015) with no earlier fit use their own and are never evaluated. No evaluated fit sees a scale estimated from its own season or later (`scales_for`, tested). A fit whose worst stored scale has R-hat above 1.1 is rejected and retried with the next seed.
